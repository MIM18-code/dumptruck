# Asset provenance

The maintainer confirmed on 2026-09-08 that these assets were created for this
project using Claude and generation tools, including his own Magnific account.
The repository's development notes attribute the static and animated SVG truck
artwork to Claude/Opus and the truck sound effects to Magnific. The maintainer
authorizes inclusion of the generated artwork and sounds in the public release.

The assets are included under the project's Apache-2.0 license to the extent
of the rights held by MindInMotion LLC and the contributors. AI generation alone
is not a reason to exclude an asset. This record relies on the maintainer's
statement; it is not an independent verification of account or generation logs.
No stock-library download or third-party artist contribution was identified
in this review. The generating services' applicable terms remain in force.
